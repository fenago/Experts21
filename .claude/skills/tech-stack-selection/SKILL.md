# Tech stack selection

## Components

POS and payments · order-ahead / online ordering · loyalty and gift cards · delivery integrations (Uber Eats, DoorDash — fees often 15–30%) · scheduling and payroll · accounting (QuickBooks/Xero) · inventory · guest Wi-Fi with email/SMS opt-in · label printer or KDS for mobile orders · music licensing service · security cameras.

## Evaluation criteria

Total monthly cost (software + hardware + processing) · card processing rate (negotiate; model assumes ~3%) · contract length and cancellation · **offline mode** (keeps selling during internet/power issues — important in hurricane season) · Spanish-language interface for staff · integrations with accounting and scheduling · data ownership and export · support hours · ease of training.
