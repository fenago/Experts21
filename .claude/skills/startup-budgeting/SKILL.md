# Startup budgeting

Use the line items and ranges in `docs/04-startup-costs.md` as the starting point.

## Line items

Lease deposit and pre-opening rent · design, engineering, permit fees · build-out · coffee and kitchen equipment · furniture, fixtures, signage · POS and tech · opening inventory · branding and launch marketing · pre-opening hiring and training · legal, accounting, licenses, insurance deposits · working-capital reserve (4–6 months of fixed costs).

## Rules

1. Give low / base / high for each line, and say which numbers are real quotes vs. estimates.
2. Add 15–20% contingency on build-out; 10% on everything else unless quoted.
3. Size by format (seated café, walk-up window/kiosk, trailer, cart, shop-in-shop) — see the format table in docs/04.
4. Build-out depends on the space: ~$75–$150/sq ft second-generation café vs. ~$200–$350/sq ft from a bare shell (Estimates). Subtract any landlord TI allowance.
5. Include costs of delay: each month of permitting delay = one more month of rent.
6. End with a **sources and uses** table: uses (the budget) vs. sources (equity, loan, TI allowance, equipment financing).
