# Funding and loan packaging

## Options (docs/04)

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|
| Owner equity / partners | Lenders expect real owner cash in the deal |
| SBA 7(a) loan | Startups often need ~10–25% owner injection; personal guarantees from 20%+ owners; confirm current SBA rules |
| SBA microloan | Smaller amounts (confirm current cap) via nonprofit intermediaries |
| Equipment financing / leasing | Cuts upfront cash; espresso machines and refrigeration |
| Landlord TI allowance, free rent | Negotiate in the lease |
| Local programs | Miami-Dade / City of Miami small-business grants and loans — check current rounds |
| Friends and family, investors | Put terms in writing (equity vs. note) |

## Lender package contents

Executive summary · owner bios and resumes · concept and menu · market and customers (docs/01–02) · competition (docs/03) · management and staffing plan · startup budget with **sources and uses** · 3-year projections (monthly year 1) · break-even and DSCR (≥ 1.25) · lease LOI · personal financial statements, credit, tax returns.

## Rules

Model debt service in the P&L (hand off to financial-modeling-analyst). Keep the 4–6 month reserve separate from build money. Don't over-borrow against optimistic sales.
