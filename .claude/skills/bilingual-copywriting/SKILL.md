# Bilingual copywriting

## Guidelines

1. Write both languages as originals, not word-for-word translations. Keep meaning and tone parallel.
2. Spanish: neutral Latin American Spanish that sounds natural in Miami (Cuban, Venezuelan, Colombian, and other communities). Avoid Spain-specific terms.
3. Use well-known Miami coffee words as-is: cafecito, colada, cortadito, café con leche.
4. Spanglish only where it fits the brand voice.
5. Short and clear: menus and signs should read in seconds.
6. Keep prices, allergens, and legal text identical in both languages.
7. Recommend a native-speaker review for anything printed or permanent.

## Mini glossary

oat milk = leche de avena · almond milk = leche de almendra · whole milk = leche entera · iced = frío / con hielo · cold brew = cold brew (widely used) · pastry = pastelito / repostería · order ahead = ordena por adelantado.

Output both versions side by side.
