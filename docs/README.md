## The short version

- **Demand is real and growing.** Florida's coffee and snack shop sector is about **$3.7B (2026, IBISWorld)**, with business counts growing ~6.5%/yr since 2021. Miami has a deep coffee culture (Cuban cafecito) plus a growing third-wave scene and a wave of new national and international entrants.
- **Competition is getting heavier.** Black Sheep Coffee (UK), The Coffee (Brazil), 7 Brew and Lola Beans are all expanding into Miami in 2025–2027, on top of Starbucks, Dunkin, strong local roasters (Panther, Per'La, Pasión del Cielo) and hundreds of ventanitas selling $2 cafecitos.
- **Rent is the main threat.** Miami-Dade retail asking rent is about **$50/sq ft/yr** on average; Brickell and Miami Beach run **$68–$70+**, and prime Wynwood asks **$100+**. Even Panther Coffee is closing its Sunset Harbour shop (Feb 2026) because of rent. Brokers' rule of thumb: rent ≤ **10% of sales**.
- **Labor costs just stepped up.** Florida's minimum wage reached **$15.00/hr on Sept 30, 2026** (tipped: $11.98). Miami barista pay runs roughly **$14–$20/hr** plus tips.
- **Budget:** a sit-down Miami café of 1,000–1,500 sq ft realistically needs **~$400K–$900K** all-in including a working-capital reserve. A kiosk, cart, trailer or ventanita-style walk-up window can start at **~$60K–$175K**.
- **Break-even (illustrative):** about **$80K–$85K/month in sales**, or roughly **340 transactions/day at an $8 average ticket**, for a mid-size shop. See the model in [05-operating-costs.md](05-operating-costs.md).
- **Biggest risks:** rent and lease terms, labor cost, green coffee price swings, hurricanes/insurance, summer slow season, crowded market, and permitting delays. See [09-risks.md](09-risks.md).

## Open questions for the next phase

These need a decision from Dr. Lee or field work before the agents can go further:

1. **Format:** full café with seating, a walk-up window/kiosk, or a roaster-café?
2. **Neighborhood:** Brickell, Wynwood, Coral Gables, Little Havana, Miami Beach, Doral, Coconut Grove, or a suburb with lower rent?
3. **Budget and funding:** how much capital, and how much from loans (SBA 7(a)) vs. own funds?
4. **Concept:** what makes this shop different (e.g., Latin American single-origins, Cuban-meets-third-wave menu, matcha/specialty non-coffee, work-friendly space)?
5. **Roasting:** buy from a local roaster (Per'La, Panther, etc.) at first, or roast in-house?
6. **Owner role:** will the owner work in the shop daily, or hire a general manager from day one?
