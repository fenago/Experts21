# 4. Startup Costs

*Research as of November 2026. See [10-sources.md](10-sources.md) for sources. Miami line items are our estimates built from national benchmarks plus Miami rent data. Get real quotes before committing.*

## National benchmarks (2025 guides)

| Format | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Kiosk / cart | ~$60K–$150K |
| Lean café | ~$75K |
| Seated café | ~$80K–$350K |
| Drive-thru only | up to ~$250K |
| Full-service café | $320K+ |

Guides also recommend keeping **4–6 months of operating costs** in reserve on top of startup costs. Miami costs run **above** these national numbers because of rent, construction prices, insurance and permitting time.

## Miami estimate — seated café, 1,000–1,500 sq ft

| Item | Low | High | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lease deposit + pre-opening rent | $20,000 | $50,000 | Often 2–3 months' rent as deposit; rent may start before opening unless free rent is negotiated |
| Architect, engineering, permit fees | $15,000 | $45,000 | Drawings for build-out, MEP, health/fire plan review |
| Build-out / construction | $100,000 | $400,000 | ~$75–$150/sq ft in a second-generation café space; ~$200–$350/sq ft from a bare shell (plumbing, grease trap, electrical, HVAC). Negotiate a tenant improvement (TI) allowance |
| Coffee & kitchen equipment | $60,000 | $130,000 | See list below |
| Furniture, fixtures, signage, décor | $20,000 | $60,000 | Signage needs a city permit |
| POS, Wi-Fi, music, cameras | $3,000 | $10,000 | Toast, Square, etc. |
| Opening inventory (coffee, milk, cups, food) | $8,000 | $15,000 | |
| Branding, website, launch marketing | $10,000 | $30,000 | |
| Hiring & training before opening | $15,000 | $30,000 | 2–3 weeks of paid training |
| Legal, accounting, licenses, insurance deposits | $8,000 | $20,000 | |
| **Subtotal (to open the doors)** | **~$260,000** | **~$790,000** | |
| Working-capital reserve (4–6 months) | $100,000 | $200,000 | Covers slow ramp-up and the summer slump |
| **Total capital needed** | **~$360,000** | **~$990,000** | Middle case ≈ **$550K–$650K** |

## Equipment list (typical)

| Item | Rough cost |
|---|---|
| Commercial espresso machine (2–3 group, e.g., La Marzocco, Slayer, Synesso) | $15,000–$35,000 |
| Espresso grinders (2–3) | $3,000–$6,000 each |
| Batch brewer + pour-over setup + bulk grinder | $4,000–$10,000 |
| Water filtration (essential with Miami's water) | $2,000–$5,000 |
| **Ice machine** (critical — iced drinks dominate) | $4,000–$8,000 |
| Refrigeration: under-counter, reach-in, display case | $10,000–$25,000 |
| Blenders, oven/turbo-chef for food | $3,000–$15,000 |
| Dishwasher, 3-compartment sink, hand sinks | $5,000–$12,000 |
| Smallwares (pitchers, scales, tampers, cups) | $2,000–$5,000 |

Leasing or financing equipment can cut upfront cash. Used espresso machines from reputable dealers can save 30–50%.

## Lower-cost formats for Miami

| Format | Estimated total | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Coffee cart for events/markets | $25,000–$60,000 | Needs mobile food license and commissary |
| Trailer or mobile truck | $60,000–$150,000 | Good for testing neighborhoods |
| Walk-up window / kiosk in a shared space | $80,000–$175,000 | Very Miami (ventanita style); low rent; no seating |
| Shop-in-shop (inside a hotel, gym, coworking) | $50,000–$150,000 | Revenue share or low fixed rent |

## Funding options

- Owner equity and partners
- **SBA 7(a) loan** (often 10–25% owner down payment for a startup)
- Equipment financing / leasing
- Landlord TI allowance and free-rent months (negotiate hard — Miami landlords are seeing more vacancies in some areas)
- Local programs: Miami-Dade and City of Miami small-business grants/loans (check current rounds)

## Timeline

Opening a seated café usually takes **6–12 months** from signing the lease: design (1–2 mo), permits (2–4+ mo in Miami), build-out (2–4 mo), hiring/training (1 mo). Every month of delay costs rent.
