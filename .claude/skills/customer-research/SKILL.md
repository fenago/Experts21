# Customer surveys and concept testing

## Survey (≤ 10 questions, English and Spanish)

Cover: how often they buy coffee out; where now and why; what they usually order; what they spend; what's missing nearby; reaction to 2–3 concept statements; price for a latte / cafecito / pastry; preferred visit time; how they find new places; age range and neighborhood.

Avoid leading questions. Randomize concept order. Aim for 100+ responses for direction (not statistical proof).

## Live tests

- **Pop-up or cart** at farmers markets, events, or a partner business: track sales by item and price point.
- **Tasting panel** (10–20 people): blind-test espresso blends and signature drinks; score 1–5.
- **Pre-sale or waitlist sign-ups** as a demand signal.

Recruit via Instagram, local groups, coworking spaces, campuses (UM, FIU, MDC). Get consent; collect no more personal data than needed.

## Summary format

Top 3 findings · segment differences · willingness to pay · concept winner and why · what to change · confidence level.
