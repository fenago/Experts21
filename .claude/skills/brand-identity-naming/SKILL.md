# Brand identity and naming

## Name criteria

Works in English and Spanish · easy to say, spell, and remember · fits the concept · not confusingly close to competitors (Panther, Per'La, Pasión del Cielo, etc.) · available.

## Availability checks

Florida Sunbiz entity/fictitious name search · USPTO trademark search · .com domain · Instagram and TikTok handles · Google search for similar Miami businesses. Flag trademark questions to business-attorney.

## Brand platform

Purpose · audience (docs/02) · positioning (docs/03 gap: specialty quality + Miami/Latin identity) · personality (3–4 words) · tone of voice in each language · story (coffee origins, founder, neighborhood).

## Identity

Logo, colors, type, cups and bags, signage, menu board, one "photo-worthy" moment in the space (mural, neon, signature cup).

Avoid cultural clichés — check references with latin-market-advisor. Present 3 options with pros/cons and a recommendation.
