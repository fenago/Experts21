# Staffing model and labor scheduling

## Inputs

Hours open · hourly sales curve (peak 7–10 AM) · menu complexity · wages (docs/07: baristas ~$16–$19/hr + tips; shift leads $18–$22; manager $50K–$70K) · payroll burden ~10–15%.

## Rules of thumb (Estimates — refine with POS data)

- Peak: 3–4 on the floor in a mid-size café; afternoons: 2. Never fewer than 2 for safety and breaks.
- Track sales per labor hour and transactions per labor hour by daypart.
- Labor target 30–35% of sales (docs/05); flag above 40%.

## Schedule

1. Forecast sales by hour for the week (last 4 weeks, adjust for season and events).
2. Assign positions by daypart (bar, register/window, floor/dish).
3. Post schedules 2 weeks ahead; respect minor-labor rules and overtime.
4. Review actual vs. scheduled weekly.

Output: headcount plan and a weekly schedule template with labor cost and labor %.
