# Florida and Miami-Dade regulatory lookup

Not legal advice. Rules differ by municipality and change — confirm with the agency.

## Step 1: Find the jurisdiction

Check the address in the Miami-Dade Property Appraiser search to see if it's in a city (Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral, etc.) or unincorporated Miami-Dade. City rules then apply on top of county and state rules.

## Step 2: The approval chain (docs/06)

1. Business setup: LLC/corp (Sunbiz), DBA if needed, EIN (IRS), sales and reemployment tax (Florida DOR), workers' comp (4+ employees).
2. Zoning and Certificate of Use check — before signing a lease. Change of use can trigger parking rules and impact fees.
3. Build-out: building permit (plans by licensed architect/engineer), fire review, grease trap (WASD/DERM FOG program), backflow, sign permit, Certificate of Occupancy/Completion.
4. Operating: DBPR Public Food Service Establishment license (seating or non-seating), Certificate of Use, county Local Business Tax Receipt, city BTR.
5. Situational: sidewalk café permit, alcohol license (DBPR ABT), music licensing, mobile food license.
