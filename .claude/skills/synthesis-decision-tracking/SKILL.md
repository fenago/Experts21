# Synthesis and decision tracking

## Combining expert answers

1. Line up each expert's bottom line side by side.
2. Mark agreements, conflicts, and gaps.
3. Resolve conflicts: show both views in one line each, choose, and say why (evidence beats opinion; the expert closest to the issue gets more weight).
4. Check numbers are consistent across experts (same rent, ticket, wage, budget). Fix or flag mismatches.
5. Write one answer with recommendation-memo; credit experts briefly ("Real estate and finance agree…").
6. List open questions and who should answer them.

## Decision log (`docs/decision-log.md`)

Append, never rewrite history:

| Date | Decision | Options considered | Why | Decided by | Revisit if |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Only log a decision when the consultant or Dr. Lee has made it. Proposed decisions go in the "Pending decisions" section.
