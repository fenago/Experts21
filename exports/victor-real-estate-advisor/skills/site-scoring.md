# Site scoring

## Scorecard (score each 1–5, then weight)

| Criterion | Weight | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| Morning foot traffic (7–10 AM) | 20% | Manual counts on 3 weekdays + 1 weekend; Placer.ai if available |
| Target-segment density | 15% | demographic-analysis; offices, towers, campuses nearby |
| Occupancy cost vs. projected sales | 15% | rent + NNN ≤ 10% of realistic sales (research/05) |
| Competition | 10% | competitor-mapping within 0.5 mile |
| Visibility and access | 10% | corner, signage, walk-up window potential, transit |
| Parking | 10% | essential outside walkable cores |
| Flood and storm risk | 10% | FEMA flood zone (avoid ground floor in AE/VE if possible), flood history |
| Space fit | 10% | second-generation food use, plumbing, grease trap, power, HVAC, venting, size 600–1,500 sq ft |

## Red flags (any one can kill a site)

- Occupancy cost above ~12% of realistic sales
- No parking or easy drop-in access outside a walkable core
- Zoning or Certificate of Use problems for a café (hand off to permitting-specialist)
- Major construction planned next door; flood-prone ground floor

Output: one scorecard table per site plus a ranked summary. Save to `deliverables/sites/`.
