# Social content planning

## Content pillars

Coffee craft · people and staff · Miami culture and neighborhood · behind the scenes (build-out, roasting, prep) · new drinks and offers · events · customer posts (reshare with permission).

## Cadence (starting point)

Instagram: 4–5 posts/week + daily stories · TikTok: 3–5 short videos/week · Google Business Profile: weekly update and photos.

## Short-video formula

Hook in the first 2 seconds → show the drink/person/moment → one line of story → call to action (visit, order ahead, follow). Use trending sounds when they fit.

## Miami calendar hooks (docs/08)

Art Basel (early Dec) · Miami Music Week / Ultra (Mar) · Noche Buena / Christmas · Three Kings Day (Jan 6) · Hispanic Heritage Month (Sept 15–Oct 15) · National Coffee Day (Sept 29) · summer local promotions · hurricane-season community posts.
