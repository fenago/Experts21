# SAMPLE landlord offer (training example) - 801 Brickell Ave, ground floor unit 104

Space: 1,150 sq ft second-generation cafe space with existing grease trap and 200A electrical
Base rent: $72 per sq ft per year, NNN
NNN charges (taxes, insurance, common area): $19 per sq ft per year
Term: 7 years
Annual increases: 4% per year
Free rent: 3 months
Tenant improvement allowance: $25 per sq ft
Security deposit: 3 months of rent
Personal guarantee: required for the full term
Exclusive use: none offered (another coffee tenant could open in the building)
Offer expires: December 15, 2026
