Experts21 design options

Three static previews of the app's main screen, built from the real team list (27 experts plus Elena), a real Brickell lease question, and Wren's top usability fixes. Each page works on a phone (tab bar) and on a desktop (sidebar). Buttons don't call the API.

Recommendation: Option A, Liquid Glass. It gives the Apple feel the project asked for, and it passes every check in this audit. Option B is the safe fallback if glass feels like too much.

Recommended

Option A: Liquid Glass

A frosted, floating glass sidebar, toolbar, and tab bar over a soft warm wash. Cards and answers stay solid.

Best for
A brand feel that looks like the newest Apple apps.
Trade-offs
Most glass to get right: needs the solid fallbacks, and more GPU work than B.
Accessibility audit
Passed: axe (WCAG 2.2 AA) zero violations in 100 runs (light, dark, increase contrast, reduced motion, forced colors, reduce-transparency and more-contrast switches; phone and desktop); every target at least 44x44px; works at 200% text; glass tint checked over white and black.

Option B: Clean Apple Light

Solid white surfaces, grouped lists, and hairlines. Glass appears only on the phone tab bar.

Best for
Reading long answers, older devices, and the lowest risk of legibility trouble.
Trade-offs
Calmer and less distinctive. Looks like a standard iOS settings app.
Accessibility audit
Passed: axe (WCAG 2.2 AA) zero violations in 100 runs (same modes as A); every target at least 44x44px; works at 200% text; glass only on the tab bar.
Open Option B (Clean Apple Light)

Option C: Dark Pro

Dark-only, macOS-style: flush dark-glass sidebar and toolbar, dense rows, amber accent, flat conversation.

Best for
Power use at a desk, long sessions, and a pro-tool feel.
Trade-offs
No light theme. Denser rows, and amber on black is a different look from Apple's blue.
Accessibility audit
Passed: axe (WCAG 2.2 AA) zero violations in 100 runs (dark-only; also tested with light preference, increase contrast, reduced motion, forced colors, and the switches); every target at least 44x44px; works at 200% text.

What changed from the live app (all options)

  • Navigation: bottom tab bar on phones (Team, Experts, History); persistent sidebar on wide screens with search, Ask the whole team pinned, Recent, then the expert groups.
  • Failure states: a failed question stays in the thread with “Not sent.” and Try again. A failed expert in a team run stays open with Retry, and Elena's answer says who is missing. Status uses an icon and words, not red alone.
  • Header: one line (name and title) with an About button for voice, working style, and model, written in plain words.
  • Type: everything is in rem, with a 17px body and a 16px minimum for the text box, so it follows text-size settings.
  • Selection: a leading bar, tint, and weight, plus aria-current, so it survives forced colors.

How to test accessibility switches

Browsers can't always emulate Reduce Transparency, so each option accepts a query string: ?a11y=reduce-transparency, ?a11y=more-contrast, ?a11y=reduce-motion (combine with commas). The real OS settings are also honored.
