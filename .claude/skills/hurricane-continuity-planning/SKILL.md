# Hurricane and business-continuity planning

Atlantic hurricane season runs **June 1 – November 30**.

## Before the season (by May)

Review insurance (wind deductible, flood, business interruption) · shutters/impact glass · generator or transfer switch for refrigeration (check lease and permits) · photo/video inventory of equipment for claims · backup suppliers · staff contact tree and pay plan during closures · cloud-based POS and data backup · emergency cash reserve.

## 72–24 hours before landfall

Bring in outdoor furniture and signs · raise equipment and stock off the floor (flood zones) · unplug and protect electronics · use or remove perishables · final payroll and staff check-in · post closure on Google, Instagram, and signage.

## After the storm

1. Staff safety first; check in with everyone.
2. Photograph damage before cleanup; notify insurer and landlord.
3. Food safety: discard temperature-controlled foods held too warm for too long during outages (follow FDA Food Code / DBPR guidance; hand off to food-safety-specialist).
4. Inspect equipment and water before reopening; flush lines and replace filters.
5. Reopen fast if safe — serving coffee to neighbors after a storm builds loyalty.

Also plan for summer heat and short power outages (AC failure, fridge failure).
