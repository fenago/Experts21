# 8. Marketing

*Research as of November 2026. See [10-sources.md](10-sources.md) for sources.*

## Positioning (draft — for the marketing agent to refine)

Most open space in the market: **specialty-grade coffee with a real Miami/Latin identity**, bilingual, fast, and welcoming. Example lines to test:

- "Third-wave coffee, Miami soul."
- "Cafecito, elevated."

Whatever the concept, it must answer: *Why come here instead of Panther, Black Sheep, Starbucks, or the ventanita next door?*

## Budget

- Independent coffee shops typically spend **3–6% of sales** on marketing.
- Plus a **launch budget of $10K–$30K** (see [04-startup-costs.md](04-startup-costs.md)).

## Channels

| Channel | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Main discovery channel for Miami food & coffee | Daily stories, drink close-ups, staff, behind the scenes; bilingual captions |
| **TikTok** | Where Gen Z and tourists find "where to go" | Short videos with trending sounds, latte art, drink reveals, staff personality |
| **Google Business Profile** | "Coffee near me" searches and Maps | Photos, hours, menu, reply to every review; ask happy customers for reviews |
| **Loyalty program / app** | Increases repeat visits; competes with Starbucks app | POS-based loyalty (Toast, Square) or branded app; reward the 5th or 10th drink |
| **Email / SMS** | Strong retention channel | Collect at checkout and Wi-Fi login; monthly news, new drinks, events |
| **Direct online ordering** | Avoids 15–30% delivery app fees | Order-ahead on own site/app; use Uber Eats/DoorDash only for reach |
| **Local press & food writers** | Miami New Times, Time Out Miami, Eater Miami, Barista Magazine, The Infatuation | Press kit and pre-opening tasting |
| **Influencers** | Miami foodie creators drive traffic quickly | Invite 10–20 micro-influencers to a soft opening |
| **Community & partnerships** | Builds loyalty | Run clubs, yoga studios, coworking spaces, galleries, schools; host latte-art throwdowns, cuppings, art shows |
| **Wholesale & catering** | Brand awareness plus revenue | Office coffee, event bars, beans in local markets |

## Launch plan

| Timing | Actions |
|---|---|
| 8–12 weeks before | Name and brand finalized; Instagram and TikTok accounts live; "coming soon" signage; start content (build-out progress) |
| 4–6 weeks before | Website with menu; email list sign-up; pitch local press; recruit influencers |
| 1–2 weeks before | Friends & family and soft opening (free or discounted); test operations; collect first reviews |
| Opening week | Grand opening event (DJ, free cafecito hour, giveaways); influencer visits; launch loyalty program |
| First 90 days | Weekly new content; respond to every review; track daily transactions and repeat rate; adjust menu |

## Seasonal calendar (Miami)

- **Nov–Apr (high season):** tourists and snowbirds; Art Basel (early Dec) and Miami Music Week (Mar) — pop-ups and special drinks.
- **May–Oct (slow season, hurricane season):** focus on locals — loyalty offers, summer iced menu, happy hour, local events.
- **Holidays:** Noche Buena/Christmas, Three Kings Day, Hispanic Heritage Month, National Coffee Day (Sept 29).

## Key metrics to track

- Daily transactions and average ticket
- Repeat-customer rate (from loyalty data)
- Google rating and review count
- Social followers and engagement; redemptions from each campaign
- Cost to acquire a customer vs. lifetime value

## Brand basics checklist

- [ ] Bilingual name and tagline that work in English and Spanish
- [ ] Logo, colors, cups, bags, signage
- [ ] "Instagrammable" spot in the shop (mural, neon, signature cup)
- [ ] Origin story of the coffee and the founder
- [ ] Signature drinks (e.g., specialty cortadito, guava cold foam, Cuban-style cold brew)
