# Competitor mapping

## Radius

~0.5 mile in dense cores; 1–2 miles in suburbs. Include all tiers from docs/03: (A) local specialty, (B) international/national specialty entrants, (C) mass chains, (D) Cuban bakeries/ventanitas, (E) substitutes.

## Fields to capture

Name · tier · distance · format (seated, window, drive-thru) · prices (espresso, latte, cold brew, cafecito) · hours · seating and Wi-Fi · parking · Google rating and review count · what they're known for · date checked.

## Mystery-shop checklist

Arrival-to-drink time · drink quality (taste, temperature, milk texture) · staff friendliness and language · cleanliness · crowd at peak · vibe and seating use · mobile-order handling · price paid.

## Output

1. Competitor table (save to `deliverables/competition/`).
2. A short gap summary: what nobody nearby does well, and where we can win (see positioning map in docs/03).
3. Watch list of new entrants (Black Sheep, The Coffee, 7 Brew, Lola Beans as of late 2026).
