# Floor plan and bar workflow

## Zones

Entry and queue → order/POS → espresso bar → milk and cold station → handoff → mobile-order pickup shelf → condiments → seating → back of house (storage, dish, office, restroom).

## Principles

1. Linear flow: order → make → hand off, without customers crossing paths.
2. Separate mobile/delivery pickup from the main handoff.
3. Barista reach: grinders, milk, ice, and cups within one or two steps.
4. Sightline from the bar to the door.
5. **Walk-up window (ventanita):** strongly consider in Miami — fast cafecito/espresso sales without entering.
6. Seating mix: power outlets for remote workers in some zones; turnover seating elsewhere. Shade and fans for outdoor seating.
7. Accessibility: ADA-compliant route, clearances, restroom, and a lowered counter section — confirm with the architect.
8. Plan for equipment heat, drains, electrical, and storage for bulk milk and ice.

Output: a zone list with approximate square feet, a simple sketch or description, and the bar station order. Hand construction details to buildout-design-advisor.
