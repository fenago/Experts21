# Recipe costing and menu pricing

## Cost per item

Item cost = Σ (ingredient quantity × unit cost) + cup + lid + sleeve/straw + napkin share.
Typical inputs (Estimates — replace with supplier prices): espresso dose 18–20 g; milk 6–12 oz by size; cold brew concentrate per recipe; alt-milk upcharge covers its higher cost.

## Targets (docs/05)

- Drinks: 22–25% COGS
- Bought-in pastries and food: 35–45% COGS
- Blended total: 28–30%

## Price setting

1. Target price = item cost ÷ target COGS %.
2. Compare with competitors near the site (competitor-mapping). Miami context: ventanitas sell cafecito very cheaply; specialty shops and chains set the latte anchor — check current prices, don't assume.
3. Round to clean price points (.25 / .50 / .00).
4. Menu engineering: classify items by margin and popularity — keep stars, re-price plowhorses, rework puzzles, drop dogs.
5. Re-cost every quarter and whenever coffee, milk, or cup prices move more than 5%.

Output: a costing table (item, ingredients, cost, price, COGS %, margin $) saved to `deliverables/menu/`.
