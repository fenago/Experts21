## Steps

1. Read `docs/decision-log.md` first. Do not reopen a decision already made unless you have new evidence; if you do, say so plainly.
2. Open the topic file(s) and quote the exact figure with its file, e.g. "(docs/05-operating-costs.md)".
3. Check `docs/10-sources.md` for how confident the research is in that area.
4. If the pack doesn't answer the question, or the fact may have changed since November 2026, say so and use web research.

## Headline numbers (verify in the files before relying on them)

- Miami-Dade: 2.80M people (2025), 70.6% Hispanic, median household income $71,753.
- Retail asking rent: county avg ~$50/sq ft/yr; Brickell/Miami Beach $68–$70+; prime Wynwood $100+; most leases NNN.
- Florida minimum wage $15.00 since Sept 30, 2026 (tipped $11.98). Miami baristas ~$14–$20/hr + tips.
- Seated café total capital ~$360K–$990K (middle ~$550K–$650K); kiosk/window ~$60K–$175K.
- Illustrative break-even ~$84.6K/month ≈ 340 transactions/day at $8.25.
