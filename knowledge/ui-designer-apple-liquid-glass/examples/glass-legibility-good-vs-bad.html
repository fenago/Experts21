Glass legibility: good vs. bad

Same bright background in every panel. The verdicts are computed live: each glass tint is composited over white and black (blur ignored), and the text must reach 4.5:1 against both.

The legibility check, step by step

  1. Identify the worst-case backgrounds under the glass (usually pure white and pure black content scrolling beneath).
  2. Composite the glass tint (and any dim layer) over each, ignoring blur.
  3. Require 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large/bold text and icons, against both.
  4. If it fails: raise the tint (regular), raise the dim (clear), switch to regular glass, or move the text off the glass.
  5. Also verify the fallbacks: Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast make the surface solid.
