# PR, influencer, and partnership outreach

## Media targets (docs/08)

Miami New Times · Time Out Miami · Eater Miami · The Infatuation · Barista Magazine · Haute Living · Miami Herald food · WLRN · local TV morning shows · neighborhood newsletters.

## Press kit

One-page story (founder, concept, coffee, neighborhood) · fact sheet (address, hours, opening date, menu highlights, prices) · 10–15 high-quality photos · founder quote · contact.

## Pitch template

Subject: specific and local. Body: 3 short paragraphs — the news, why it matters to Miami readers, the invitation (preview tasting date). Follow up once after 5–7 days.

## Influencers

Focus on Miami micro-creators (roughly 5K–50K followers) in food, coffee, and neighborhood content. Invite 10–20 to a soft opening. Paid or gifted posts must be disclosed (#ad / #gifted, FTC rules).

## Partnerships and events

Run clubs, yoga/fitness studios, coworking spaces, galleries, hotels, UM/FIU/MDC student groups. Events: latte-art throwdowns, cuppings, art shows, community mornings.

Track: outlet/creator, date contacted, response, coverage, visits/redemptions.
