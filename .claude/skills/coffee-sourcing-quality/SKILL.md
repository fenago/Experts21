# Coffee sourcing and quality

## Roaster evaluation

| Criterion | What to check |
|---|---|
| Quality | Cupping scores (specialty = 80+ on the SCA scale), espresso performance in milk and straight |
| Consistency | Same coffee across 3 deliveries |
| Price | $/lb by volume tier; price lock options |
| Terms | Minimums, delivery frequency, roast-date freshness, payment terms |
| Support | Barista training, equipment programs (watch for equipment-tied contracts), tech service |
| Story | Origin transparency, Latin American origins that fit the concept |

Local options to evaluate include Per'La and Panther (wholesale) and others (docs/03). Note: a local roaster may also be a competitor.

## Price risk (docs/01, docs/10)

Green coffee hit records in 2025; U.S. tariffs on Brazilian coffee were removed Nov 2025, but prices stayed high into late 2026. Track ICE arabica "KC" futures. Ask for 6–12 month fixed-price contracts. Roaster price changes lag futures by ~4–6 months.

## In-house roasting decision

Consider only after steady volume. Factors: roaster cost and venting/permits, space, skill and time, green buying, and whether wholesale sales can use the extra capacity. Model the break-even volume before deciding.

## Cupping steps

Standard SCA protocol: same roast level, 8.25 g per 150 ml, 93 °C water, break at 4 min, score aroma, flavor, aftertaste, acidity, body, balance, overall. Blind the samples.
