# Lease analysis and negotiation

## Total occupancy cost

Base rent + NNN (property tax, insurance, CAM) + any percentage rent + marketing fund. Target ≤ 10% of realistic sales (docs/05). Miami asking rents: county ~$50; Brickell/Miami Beach $68–$70+; prime Wynwood $100+ per sq ft/yr; NNN often adds $15–$30.

## Compare offers in one table

Size · base rent/sq ft · NNN estimate · monthly total · escalations (%/yr) · term and options · free rent · TI allowance · rent start trigger · deposit · personal guarantee · use clause · exclusivity · total cost over the term.

## Standard asks for a café tenant

1. Rent starts at opening (or X days after permits), not at signing.
2. 3–6 months free rent; TI allowance toward build-out.
3. Contingency: lease voidable if zoning, Certificate of Use, or permits are denied.
4. Exclusive-use clause: no other coffee seller in the center.
5. Cap on NNN/CAM increases (e.g., 3–5%/yr on controllable costs).
6. Limited or burning-off personal guarantee.
7. Renewal options at pre-set increases; right to assign/sublet on sale of business.
8. Rent abatement after hurricane/casualty damage; clear repair responsibilities for HVAC, roof, grease trap.
9. Signage rights; outdoor seating rights; hours and access.

Not legal advice. Every lease goes to `business-attorney` before signing.
