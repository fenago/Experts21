# Recruiting and training

## Job post (English and Spanish)

Role · pay range (be transparent; above $15 minimum) · tips · schedule · what a great day looks like · requirements (bilingual preferred, early mornings) · perks (free drinks, training, growth path) · how to apply.

Channels: Indeed, Culinary Agents, local coffee Instagram community, MDC and FIU hospitality programs, barista competitions (docs/07).

## Interview scorecard (1–5 each)

Hospitality · speed under pressure · reliability/availability · coffee curiosity · teamwork · English/Spanish. Use a paid working interview for finalists.

## Onboarding checklist

I-9 (and E-Verify if 25+ employees) · W-4 · handbook and tip policy · food handler training · uniform · POS login · first-week shadow schedule.

## Training plan

- Pre-opening: 2–3 weeks paid — espresso, milk, menu, POS, cleaning, service, food safety.
- Certifications: food handler for all; Certified Food Protection Manager for leads (within 30 days).
- Ongoing: weekly cupping and calibration; SCA barista courses or roaster training; skill levels barista → lead → manager.
