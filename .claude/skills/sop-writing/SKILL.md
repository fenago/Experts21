# SOP and checklist writing

## SOP format

**Title** · Purpose (1 line) · When · Who · Tools/supplies · Steps (numbered, one action each) · Standards (time, temperature, quality) · Common mistakes · Sign-off/log.

Write at a simple reading level. Provide English and Spanish versions. Use photos where helpful.

## SOPs a café needs

Opening · closing · espresso dial-in and calibration · milk steaming and texture · batch brew and cold brew · cleaning (daily, weekly, monthly) · cash handling and tips · mobile and delivery orders · receiving and storage · temperature logs · allergen handling · customer complaints and refunds · hurricane prep and reopening · new-hire onboarding.

Save to `deliverables/operations/sops/`. Keep one file per SOP; version with dates.
