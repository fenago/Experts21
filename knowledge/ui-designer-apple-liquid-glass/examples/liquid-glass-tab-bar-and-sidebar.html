Why it's built this way

  1. Tokens hold every color, with light, dark, and increased-contrast values (HIG Color).
  2. System font stack; SF Pro is never embedded (Apple's font license forbids website use).
  3. Regular glass is tinted at 72% so black text passes 4.5:1 even over pure black content, ignoring blur.
  4. Concentric corners: selected pill radius = bar radius − padding.
  5. Content (hero, cards) is solid — "Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer."
  6. -webkit-backdrop-filter for older Safari; glass needs a partly transparent background (MDN).
  7. Every target is at least 44×44px (HIG: 44×44pt on iOS).
  8. Selection = accent color + semibold + pill + aria-current — never color alone.
  9. Phones: floating tab bar at the bottom, icon + one-word label, respects the safe area.
  10. Wide screens: the same destinations become a sidebar ("Consider adapting your tab bar into a sidebar automatically").
  11. Fallbacks: no backdrop-filter → solid; Reduce Transparency → solid; Increase Contrast → solid + 2px border + darker text; forced colors → system colors.
  12. Reduce Motion: no animated selection or blur.
