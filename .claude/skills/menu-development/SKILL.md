# Menu development

## Structure (keep the core to ~15–20 items for speed)

1. **Espresso classics:** espresso, cortado, cappuccino, latte, americano, flat white.
2. **Latin specialty:** specialty-grade cafecito, cortadito, colada, café con leche — the Miami bridge (docs/03 gap).
3. **Brewed:** batch brew, rotating pour-over single-origin.
4. **Iced and cold brew:** big year-round share in Miami's heat.
5. **Non-coffee:** matcha, chai, refreshers, hot chocolate.
6. **Signatures:** 3–4 drinks with Miami flavors (e.g., guava, dulce de leche, coconut) — test before launch.
7. **Food:** pastelitos (guava/cheese), croquetas, empanadas, pastries, toast — source from local bakeries first.
8. **Retail:** whole beans, merch.

Offer alt milks and low-sugar options; label allergens.

## Recipe spec card

Name (EN/ES) · cup and size · dose · yield · brew time · water temp · milk type and amount · sweetener · ice · garnish · build order · photo · cost (recipe-costing-pricing).

## Process

Draft → cost → tasting panel (score 1–5) → adjust → time it on the bar (target speed) → finalize. Seasonal limited-time drinks 4–6 times a year.
