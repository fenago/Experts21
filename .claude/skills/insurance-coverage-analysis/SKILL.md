# Insurance coverage analysis

Not insurance advice — use a licensed independent Florida broker.

## Coverages to check

- **Business owner's policy (BOP):** property, general liability, business income/interruption.
- **Windstorm/hurricane:** often a named-storm deductible of 2–15% of insured value (docs/05). Check if wind is excluded and needs separate coverage.
- **Flood:** excluded from standard policies — buy commercial flood (NFIP or private). Check FEMA zone.
- **Workers' comp:** required with 4+ employees.
- **Equipment breakdown and spoilage:** espresso machines, refrigeration, power outages.
- **Other:** liquor liability (if alcohol), cyber (card data), employment practices (EPLI), umbrella, hired/non-owned auto (deliveries).
- **Lease requirements:** limits and additional-insured wording the landlord requires.
