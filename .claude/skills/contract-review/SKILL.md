# Contract review

Not legal advice — flag issues and send material contracts to a licensed Florida attorney.

## Checklist

Parties and signer authority · term, auto-renewal, notice windows · price, escalators, minimums (roasters often require minimum monthly purchases) · equipment tied to a supply contract (who owns it, buyout at exit) · payment terms and late fees · termination rights and fees · exclusivity · warranty and service response times (Miami service tech availability) · liability caps and indemnity · insurance requirements · data ownership and export (POS, loyalty) · personal guarantee · assignment on sale · governing law (Florida), venue, arbitration · force majeure (hurricanes, power loss).

## Output

| Clause | Rating | What it means | Suggested change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Auto-renewal 3 yrs | 🔴 Red | Locks you in unless you cancel 90 days ahead | 1-yr renewals, 30-day notice |

Red = must change or walk away · Yellow = negotiate · Green = acceptable. End with the top 3 changes to request.
