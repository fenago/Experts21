## Customer segments

| Segment | Where they are | What they want | Spend & habits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Office workers & young professionals** | Brickell, Downtown, Coral Gables, Doral | Speed in the morning, mobile order-ahead, consistent quality, a place to meet | High frequency (3–5x/week), loyalty apps work well |
| **Latin coffee lovers (Cuban, Venezuelan, Colombian, etc.)** | Everywhere, especially Little Havana, Hialeah, Westchester, Doral, Kendall | Strong, sweet coffee they know; respect for cafecito; fair price | Very price-sensitive for cafecito; open to specialty if it tastes familiar |
| **Remote workers, creatives, tech transplants** | Wynwood, Edgewater, Midtown, Coconut Grove, Brickell | Wi-Fi, outlets, good seating, single-origin and pour-over | Long stays, lower spend per hour — needs seating policy |
| **Tourists & visitors** | Miami Beach, Wynwood, Design District, Brickell | "Instagrammable" place and drinks, a local story, merch | One-time but high ticket; peaks Nov–Apr |
| **Gen Z & students** | Near UM (Coral Gables), FIU (west), Miami Dade College (Downtown and campuses) | Iced drinks, matcha, sweet signatures, trends seen on TikTok | Lower budget, very social-media driven |
| **Wellness & fitness crowd** | Miami Beach, Brickell, Coconut Grove | Oat/alt milks, low sugar, protein, matcha, healthy food | Will pay a premium for "clean" options |
| **Neighborhood families & retirees** | Coral Gables, Kendall, Pinecrest, Coconut Grove | Friendly service, pastries, parking, weekend visits | Steady, loyal, weekend-heavy |

## Recommended primary targets (to confirm)

Pick **one primary** and **one or two secondary** segments based on the site:

- **Brickell / Downtown site:** primary = professionals; secondary = residents of the high-rises, tourists.
- **Wynwood / Midtown / Edgewater site:** primary = creatives & remote workers; secondary = tourists, Gen Z.
- **Coral Gables site:** primary = professionals and UM students; secondary = families.
- **Suburban site (Doral, Kendall, Westchester):** primary = Latin coffee lovers and families; secondary = commuters (drive-thru or walk-up window helps).

## What customers expect in Miami specifically

- **Iced everything.** Heat means cold drinks sell year-round.
- **Bilingual, warm service.** Speed plus personality.
- **A Latin nod on the menu.** A specialty-grade cafecito, cortadito or colada, guava/cheese pastelitos, dulce de leche, etc.
- **Air conditioning and shade.** Outdoor seating only works with shade, fans, and in the cooler months.
- **Parking or easy access.** Outside the walkable cores, no parking can kill a café.
- **Digital ordering.** Order-ahead and delivery (Uber Eats, DoorDash) for office areas.
