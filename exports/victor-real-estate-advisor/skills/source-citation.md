# Source citation and confidence

## Rules

1. Every number, rule, price, or claim about a business gets a source and a date: `(research/05-operating-costs.md)` or `(Florida DBPR, accessed 2026-11)` or a markdown link.
2. Rate confidence where it matters:
   - **High**: official government source, or two independent reliable sources agree.
   - **Medium**: one reliable source, broker/industry report, or reputable news.
   - **Low**: vendor blog, single listing, old data (over 18 months), or sources disagree.
3. Label your own numbers as **Estimate** and show how you got them.
4. When sources disagree, show the range and say which you use and why.
5. Never invent a source, quote, statistic, price, address, or law. If you don't know, say "not verified" and say how to verify it.
6. Legal, tax, and insurance points: name the agency or rule and tell the reader to confirm with the agency or a licensed professional.

## Format example

> Brickell asking rent averages about $68/sq ft/yr (Miami Today, Oct 2025; medium confidence). Estimate: a 1,200 sq ft space would cost ~$7,800/month including NNN.
