# Demographic and census analysis

## County baseline (research/02)

2.80M people; 70.6% Hispanic; 54.5% foreign-born; 75.3% speak a language other than English at home; median household income $71,753; mean commute 30.9 min.

## Steps for a neighborhood or site

1. Define the trade area: ~0.5 mile (10-min walk) in dense cores (Brickell, Downtown, Wynwood, Miami Beach); 1–3 miles / 5–10 min drive in suburbs.
2. Pull ACS 5-year data at census tract level (data.census.gov): population, age 20–44 share, median income, Hispanic share, language, households.
3. Add **daytime population** (workers): Census OnTheMap (LEHD) or office inventory — critical for morning coffee.
4. Note students (UM, FIU, Miami Dade College campuses), hotel rooms, and residential towers under construction.
5. Compare each area to the county baseline in one table.
6. Map results to the customer segments in research/02 and say which segment each area favors.

Always give the data year; ACS figures are multi-year estimates.
