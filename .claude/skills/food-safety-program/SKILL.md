# Food safety program

Florida DBPR (Division of Hotels and Restaurants) licenses and inspects food service establishments. Confirm current rules with DBPR.

## Staff certifications (docs/06)

- Certified Food Protection Manager: pass an approved exam within 30 days of employment; plan for more than one.
- All food employees: DBPR-approved food handler training (e.g., SafeStaff, English/Spanish); renew every 3 years.

## Daily controls (FDA Food Code basics — verify with DBPR)

Cold holding at or below 41 °F · hot holding at or above 135 °F · date-label ready-to-eat items (7-day max) · milk and alt-milk rotation (first in, first out) · sanitizer concentration checks · handwashing sink always stocked and unblocked · ice scoop stored outside the ice · allergen separation · pest control contract.

## Logs

Fridge temperatures (2× daily) · cleaning schedule · sanitizer tests · receiving temperatures · staff certificates on file.

## Inspection readiness

Licenses posted · certificates on file · no expired/unlabeled items · clean steam wands and pitchers · hand sink access · thermometers in every fridge. Run a monthly mock inspection.
