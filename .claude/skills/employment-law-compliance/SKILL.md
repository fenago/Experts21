# Employment-law compliance

Not legal advice — confirm with an employment attorney or the agency.

## Key rules (docs/07; verify current values)

- **Minimum wage:** $15.00/hr since Sept 30, 2026. Annual inflation adjustments follow — confirm the next effective date.
- **Tipped minimum:** $11.98/hr (tip credit $3.02), only if tips bring the worker to at least $15.00; employer must make up any shortfall.
- **Tips:** tip pools allowed among employees; managers, supervisors, and owners may not keep employee tips (FLSA).
- **Overtime:** 1.5× regular rate over 40 hours/week (FLSA).
- **Hiring:** Form I-9 for every hire; Florida requires E-Verify for private employers with 25+ employees.
- **Workers' comp:** required for non-construction employers with 4+ employees.
- **Minors (under 18):** Florida limits hours and tasks — check before scheduling students.
- **Classification:** baristas are employees, not contractors.
- **Posters:** federal and Florida labor law posters displayed.

A checklist with each rule, how the café complies, and who owns it. Flag anything that needs an attorney.
