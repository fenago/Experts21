# Tax and bookkeeping setup

Not tax advice — confirm with a licensed CPA.

## Registrations

EIN (IRS) · Florida DOR: sales and use tax + reemployment tax · Sunbiz annual report (Jan 1–May 1) · county/city BTRs (annual).

## Recurring filings (verify frequency with DOR/IRS)

- **Sales tax:** 7% in Miami-Dade (6% state + 1% county surtax) on food and drink sold for consumption; frequency set by DOR (monthly/quarterly/annual).
- **Reemployment tax:** quarterly to Florida DOR.
- **Federal payroll:** Form 941 quarterly, 940 annually, W-2s; tip reporting rules for food businesses (ask CPA about Form 8027 and the FICA tip credit).
- **Tangible personal property return** to the county property appraiser for equipment (annual — confirm deadline).
- Florida has no personal income tax; C-corporations pay Florida corporate income tax.

## Chart of accounts (café)

Sales: espresso drinks, brewed/cold coffee, non-coffee drinks, food, retail beans, merch, catering/wholesale · COGS: coffee, dairy/alt-milk, food, packaging · Labor: hourly, salaried, payroll taxes, benefits · Occupancy: rent, NNN/CAM · Utilities · Marketing · Card fees · Repairs · Insurance · Software · Professional fees · Debt service.

Close the books monthly; reconcile POS sales, card deposits, and tips.
